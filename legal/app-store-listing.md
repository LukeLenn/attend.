## Description

attend. guides you through a short daily listening practice that trains where your attention goes.

Close your eyes. A calm voice moves you around a soundscape: a clock near you on the left, birdsong far away, rain just ahead. First you stay with one sound at a time. Then you switch quickly between sounds and places. Then you take in everything at once.

Before and after each session you rate where your attention sits, between the world around you and yourself. Over the weeks, your log shows the days you practised and how your attention shifts.

• Twelve sounds, each placed near or far, left, ahead or right
• Add real sounds from your own room
• Choose the full session or just one part, and set each part's length
• Spoken guidance, so your eyes can stay closed
• A practice log you can share with your therapist
• Gentle daily reminder, if you want one
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no tracking.

attend. is designed to support attention training set as homework by a therapist. It is not medical advice or a treatment.
