# attend. — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

attend. is an attention training app. It is built so that your information stays with you.

## What the app stores

- Your practice sessions: date and time, length, which parts you did, how many sounds you used, your before and after self-attention ratings, and any notes you write.
- Your settings: chosen sounds and their positions, session lengths, voice, speaking speed, volume and reminder time.

All of this is stored only on your device. We don't run servers that receive it, and we can't see it.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or email.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No microphone access. Sounds are generated on the device.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

## Sharing you choose to do

If you tap “share summary” or “export csv”, the app hands the text or file to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

## Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Turning them off cancels them.

## Deleting your data

Settings → “Delete all data” erases everything the app has stored. Deleting the app also removes it.

## Not medical advice

attend. supports attention training practice. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional.
